Studio Culture

No judgment. Just heat and hard work.

Kula Yoga Studio is a friendly environment filled with laughter, honesty, camaraderie, and appreciation for hard work. Whether you're a first-timer or a longtime student, our enthusiastic approach to teaching will have you coming back for more.

Amenities

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

A retail space with quality yoga clothing, mats, towels, and accessories. Mat and towel rentals available for class. Beverages to re-hydrate after you sweat it out. Clean, private men's and women's locker rooms with showers — so you can head back out the door looking as good as you feel.
