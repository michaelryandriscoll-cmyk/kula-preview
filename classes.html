26 & 2

Hot Yoga

Our Hot Yoga series is a routine of 26 postures and 2 breathing exercises that works the whole body in 90 minutes. Practiced in a heated room, the postures work synergistically and cumulatively to return the body to a balanced state — building energy, stamina, and flexibility with every class.

The heat increases flexibility, reduces the risk of injury, and lets you reach deeper into every pose — while the structured sequence builds a strong mind-body connection that carries off the mat.

  • Increases flexibility & reduces injury risk
  • Builds strength, stamina & full-body tone
  • Improves spine flexibility & posture
  • Boosts circulation & lung capacity
  • Relieves stress, sharpens mental clarity
  • Supports detox, digestion & immunity

Inferno

Hot Pilates

Inferno Hot Pilates is a fun, challenging, full-body workout built on Pilates principles — low-impact, high-intensity, and performed on a mat and towel in a heated room with energetic music.

It strengthens and tones muscle and burns fat without the pounding of high-impact training, and it's designed to meet every fitness level where they are.

  • Full-body strength & toning
  • Low-impact on joints
  • High-intensity calorie burn
  • Boosts metabolism & endurance
  • Improves core stability
  • All fitness levels welcome
